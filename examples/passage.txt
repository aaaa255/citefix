Transformers dispense with recurrence and convolutions and rely entirely on attention mechanisms [1]. Residual connections made it possible to train networks that are much deeper than earlier architectures [2]. Batch normalization reduces internal covariate shift and allows much higher learning rates [3]. Retrieval-augmented generation combines a parametric seq2seq model with a dense vector index of Wikipedia [4].
